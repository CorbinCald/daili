Moonshine v2 streaming-tiny and streaming-small (English)

Daili's optional voice entry uses the English Moonshine v2 streaming-tiny model as its standard tier and offers streaming-small as an opt-in accuracy tier. Both come from Moonshine AI.

  • Copyright © 2025 Useful Sensors, Inc. (dba Moonshine AI)
  • License for the English-language model: MIT License
  • For the standard tier, Daili converted the original floating-point ONNX graphs to ONNX Runtime format for local mobile inference, quantizing four of the five graphs; the audio frontend is kept at full floating-point precision for accuracy.
  • The accuracy tier derives from Moonshine Voice v0.1.5's published small-streaming-en/quantized_26_08_21 model, whose audio frontend is stored as 8-bit codes and expanded to floating point on the device when the model loads. Daili stores the tied decoder embedding once instead of serializing its exact transpose twice and retains the adapter positions reachable under Daili's bounded listening duration. Daili calibrates selected weight matrices to 64-value integer palettes and retains 50 sensitive matrices at their original precision. The frontend, embeddings, tokenizer, scales, and inference operators are retained. The resulting model is delivered in reversible DMP1 archives and expanded locally. Calibration changes weights; archive decoding restores those calibrated weights exactly.
