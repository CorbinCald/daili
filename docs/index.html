Daili — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Daili is a daily budgeting app developed and published by Corbin Caldwell. Daili has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server. Your budget records, receipt images, and microphone audio are not uploaded.

Information stored on your device

Daili stores the following in the app's private storage so its features work:

  • Your monthly budget and onboarding choices
  • Your recurring expenses
  • The expenses you log, including amount, category, description, date, and time
  • Photos you optionally capture for expenses or scanned receipts
  • App preferences, such as theme, reminders, and enabled AI features
  • Optional receipt-reading and speech-recognition model files

This information is processed locally and is not sent to the developer or another company. Android cloud backup is disabled for Daili, so the operating system is instructed not to back up this app data to Google Drive.

Optional model downloads

Receipt scanning and voice entry use machine-learning models that run on your device. When you turn either feature on, Daili downloads the required model files over HTTPS from Daili's GitHub Releases. No budget records, expense details, photos, receipt text, or microphone audio are included in those requests.

As with a normal file download, GitHub may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and user agent, to deliver the files. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Daili does not use these requests to identify or track you.

Attribution and license terms for these optional files are available on the Model Licenses and Notices page.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Daili contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service. User-created data is accessed and processed only on your device.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): used when you choose to scan a receipt or attach a photo to an expense. Receipt recognition runs on your device; images are not uploaded.
  • Microphone (optional): used when you choose to add an expense by voice. Audio is processed in memory by the on-device speech model and is not saved or uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): used for reminders and Morning Rundown alerts that you enable. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device; Daili does not use a developer-operated push service.
  • Network access: used only to download the optional on-device model files described above.

You can use Daili's core manual budgeting features without camera or microphone access.

Security

Daili keeps your records in Android's app-private storage, disables Android cloud backup, and uses HTTPS for optional model downloads. Other apps cannot normally access Daili's private files unless the device's security has been compromised.

Retention and deletion

Budget and expense records remain on your device until you delete them or reset Daili. Turning off an AI feature removes that feature's downloaded model files. The in-app Reset app action removes your saved budget, expenses, recurring expenses, attached and cached photos, downloaded models, notification schedules, and settings. Clearing Daili's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app also removes its local files. The developer cannot recover any of this information because the developer never receives it.

Daili does not create user accounts, so there is no separate account to delete.

Children

Daili does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Parents and guardians should decide whether a budgeting app is appropriate for a child.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and its "Last updated" date will be revised before that version is released.
