Daili — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

Daili is a daily budgeting app developed and published by Corbin Caldwell. Daili has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or app backend that receives your financial records. Daili itself does not upload your budget records, receipt images, or microphone audio. If you explicitly turn on Android backup, Android may back up the limited data described below to your Google account.

Information stored on your device

Daili stores the following in the app's private storage so its features work:

  • Your monthly budget and onboarding choices
  • Your recurring expenses
  • The expenses you log, including amount, category, description, date, and time
  • Photos you optionally capture for expenses or scanned receipts
  • App preferences, such as theme, reminders, and enabled AI features
  • Optional receipt-reading and speech-recognition model files

This information is processed locally and is not sent to the developer. The only optional transfer of financial records is the Android backup you can choose below.

Optional encrypted Android backup

In Daili 1.13.0, Android cloud backup is disabled. The optional backup behavior described here applies to newer releases, including test builds, that offer Data backup in Settings.

Android backup is off unless you choose to turn it on. After you successfully add your fifth expense, Daili waits two seconds and offers the choice once. Choosing Keep backup off or tapping outside the prompt leaves it off. You can also turn it on or off at any time under Data backup in Settings.

When enabled, Daili permits Android to back up only the SQLite ledger and settings, supported migration data, and the backup-consent setting. This can include budgets, expenses, monthly plan history, recurring expenses, reminders, and app preferences. Cloud backup requires Android's client-side encryption capabilities, which normally require device backup and a screen lock to be enabled. The backup belongs to your Google account; Daili's developer cannot view or retrieve it. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy.

Receipt and expense photos, downloaded AI models, caches, temporary camera or audio files, and logs are excluded from cloud backup. Android's direct device-to-device transfer may include app-private receipt photos. If a cloud-restored expense refers to an excluded photo, Daili removes the unavailable photo reference while preserving the expense.

Android schedules Auto Backup asynchronously, only while its device and account requirements are met, and limits each app's cloud backup to 25 MB. A backup may be older than your newest changes, unavailable, or unable to complete, so this feature is not an immediate or unconditional backup guarantee. Turning the feature off stops Daili from contributing financial data to future backups. Declining the initial offer emits no Daili backup data. If you previously enabled backup and then turn it off, Daili retains only a small off/reset marker so a later completed Android backup can replace an older ledger snapshot; an older snapshot may remain until that pass completes or you remove it through Android or Google backup settings.

Optional model downloads

Receipt scanning and voice entry use machine-learning models that run on your device. When you turn either feature on, Daili downloads the required model files over HTTPS from Daili's GitHub Releases. If a file cannot be fetched from GitHub, Daili retries it from a public, read-only Amazon S3 bucket the developer maintains as a mirror. That bucket serves the same model files and does not accept uploads from the app. No budget records, expense details, photos, receipt text, or microphone audio are included in requests to either host.

As with any file download, the host serving the file may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address and user agent, in order to deliver it. GitHub handles that information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement, and Amazon Web Services handles it under the AWS Privacy Notice. Daili does not use these requests to identify or track you.

Attribution and license terms for these optional files are available on the Model Licenses and Notices page.

Data collection and sharing

Daili does not automatically collect, sell, or share your personal data. It contains no advertising, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting service. User-created data is accessed and processed on your device, except for the optional Android backup transfer described above, which is controlled by Android and your Google account.

Optional browser search and support email

Currency conversion uses rates stored on your device or entered by you. If you choose Search in Browser, Daili opens a Google search for the two selected currency codes and an exchange rate for one unit. Your budget, expenses, and entered amounts are not included. Google handles the search under the Google Privacy Policy.

If you choose Report problem on a storage-error screen, Daili opens an email draft containing the storage phase and error code, without ledger contents. You can review, edit, or discard it. If you send it or otherwise email the developer, your email provider delivers your email address and the message you choose to send. Daili does not send support reports automatically.

Optional Play Store rating

Daili never displays an automatic review prompt. If you choose the Rate Daili link in Settings, Daili opens its Play Store review page. If you submit a rating or written review, Google Play—not Daili—handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy. No budget, expense, photo, receipt, or microphone data is included in the request.

Permissions

  • Camera (optional): used when you choose to scan a receipt or attach a photo to an expense. Receipt recognition runs on your device; images are not uploaded.
  • Microphone (optional): used when you choose to add an expense by voice. Audio is processed in memory by the on-device speech model and is not saved or uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional): used for reminders and Morning Rundown alerts that you enable. These notifications are scheduled locally on your device; Daili does not use a developer-operated push service.
  • Network access: used to download the optional on-device model files described above. Links you choose can also open the privacy policy, model notices, Google Play rating page, browser search, or your email app.

You can use Daili's core manual budgeting features without camera or microphone access.

Security

Daili keeps your records in Android's app-private storage and uses HTTPS for optional model downloads. If you enable cloud backup, Daili requires Android's client-side encryption capability and limits backup to an explicit file allowlist. Other apps cannot normally access Daili's private files unless the device's security has been compromised.

Retention and deletion

Budget and expense records remain on your device until you delete them or reset Daili. Turning off an AI feature removes that feature's downloaded model files. The in-app Reset app action removes your saved budget, expenses, recurring expenses, attached and cached photos, downloaded models, notification schedules, and settings. Reset also turns off Daili's backup gate before deleting the ledger and, if backup had been enabled, stores only the off/reset marker described above. An earlier snapshot may remain with Google until Android completes a later device backup or you remove it through the device's backup settings. Clearing Daili's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes local files, but a previously enabled Android backup may restore included data on reinstall. You can remove stored device backups through Android or Google backup settings. The developer cannot recover any of this information because the developer never receives or controls the backup.

Daili does not create user accounts, so there is no separate account to delete.

Children

Daili does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else. Parents and guardians should decide whether a budgeting app is appropriate for a child.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page and its "Last updated" date will be revised before that version is released.
