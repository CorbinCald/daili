Website privacy

Updated September 20, 2026.

Your example stays here.

The daily allowance calculators and the simulated Daili app run in your browser. This website does not send their inputs to a server or save them in cookies or browser storage; the simulation starts over with sample data whenever the page reloads.

Your appearance preference.

Auto follows your device’s day or night appearance. If you choose Day or Night, this website saves only that preference in your browser’s local storage so it applies on your next visit. Choosing Auto removes the saved preference. This setting is not sent to a server.

No advertising scripts.

This website includes no analytics, advertising pixels, remote fonts, or third-party scripts. The hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, including your IP address and requested URL, to serve the site. Its own privacy terms apply.

When you open Google Play.

Our links can include general campaign labels such as “google” and “daili_relaunch_2026_today”, so Google Play can report aggregate campaign results. No calculator values, personal details, or unique advertising click IDs are added to these links. Google may combine the visit with information it already holds under the Google Privacy Policy.

The Android app.

This page describes the website. Read the separate Daili app privacy policy for the installed app, local data, and optional model downloads.
