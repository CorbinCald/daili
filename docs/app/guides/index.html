Try the daily allowance idea, see what works offline, and learn the different ways to record a purchase. These guides explain how Daili works so you can decide whether it fits.

Daily budget calculator ↗

Calculate a starting daily allowance from your monthly spending cap and recurring expenses. Try a free, private calculator and see how Daili handles rollover.

Offline budgeting ↗

Use Daili to track expenses offline without linking a bank or creating an account. Learn what stays on your phone and what needs an initial download.

One-time purchase ↗

Daili is a one-time purchase budget app for Android, with no subscription or in-app purchases. See what is included and check your local Google Play price.

Receipt scanning ↗

Scan receipts into your daily budget with Daili. Review merchant, total and line items before saving. Optional recognition runs on your Android phone after setup.

Voice expense entry ↗

Speak an expense in English, review the recognized details and save it in Daili. Optional voice entry works on your Android phone after a model download.
