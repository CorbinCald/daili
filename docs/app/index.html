Ask the developer ↗

Is Daili a subscription?+

No. Daili is a one-time purchase: $4.99 in the US at our last price check. Google Play shows the current price, currency, and any applicable taxes for your region. There are no in-app purchases.

Does Daili connect to my bank?+

No bank connection or Daili account is needed. You choose a monthly plan and add your expenses. Daili gives you a daily allowance based on the information you enter.

Can I use it without an internet connection?+

Yes. Manual budgeting works offline. Optional receipt scanning and voice entry need an initial model download. The models then process your entries on your device. Reinstalling or re-enabling a feature can require another download.

How do voice and receipt entry work?+

Enable the feature you want in Settings and download its model. Voice entry supports English. Scan a receipt or speak an expense, then review the result before saving. Recognition can make mistakes; check amounts and details.

What happens when I go over my allowance?+

Daili carries underspending or overspending forward as rollover. Your next day’s available amount reflects it, so your monthly plan stays in view. The allowance reflects what you enter; it is not a guarantee of what you can afford.

Which phones can use Daili?+

Daili is available for Android 8.0 and later. Google Play checks compatibility with your specific device. Optional AI features require additional storage and their speed varies by device. Daili is not available for iPhone.

What about my data?+

The developer does not receive your budget records, photos, or microphone audio. Review the app privacy policy for local storage, model downloads, deletion, and the data behavior of your installed version.
