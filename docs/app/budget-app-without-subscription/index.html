Daili/Guides/One-time purchase

A daily budget app. One purchase.

Daili is a one-time purchase for Android, with no subscription, in-app purchases or premium tier. The last checked US price was $4.99; Google Play shows the current price, currency and any applicable taxes for your region.

By Corbin Caldwell, Daili developer · Updated September 20, 2026

What is included?

Make a monthly spending plan, account for recurring expenses and see a daily allowance with rollover. Log everyday purchases, review spending history and look at the categories behind your spending pace. Optional receipt scanning and English voice expense entry are included, with an initial model download required for each feature.

There are no in-app advertisements. You do not need a Daili account or a connection to your bank. Manual budgeting works offline, and optional recognition runs on your phone after its model is downloaded.

Check the fit as well as the price.

Daili is built around a plan you enter and purchases you record. If you want automatic transaction feeds from your bank, a browser-based account dashboard or an iPhone app, Daili does not offer those. The website’s phone is an interactive demo with sample data, not an online copy of your budget.

Try the daily allowance calculator and the interactive app demo before buying. The calculator shows the basic idea; the app adds your expense history, dated plans and rollover.

What to check before installing.

Open the official Google Play listing to confirm your local price and device compatibility. Daili supports Android 8.0 and later, subject to Google Play’s checks for your phone. Optional AI features need extra storage and run at different speeds on different devices.

A one-time purchase describes how Daili is sold. Your purchase, reinstalls and any refund request are handled through Google Play and its applicable terms. Daili does not ask for card details inside the app.

Questions about one-time purchase

Does Daili have a monthly or annual fee?

No. Daili is sold as a one-time purchase, with no recurring subscription or in-app purchases.

Do receipt scanning and voice entry cost extra?

No extra Daili purchase is required. They are optional features that need an initial model download and additional device storage.
